Privacy Policy

Youtube Multiselect · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Scope

This policy explains what Youtube Multiselect processes when you use its multi-selection and batch-management tools on YouTube and YouTube Music. The extension is designed to work locally in your browser and does not require an account with us.

Information processed

The extension temporarily processes video-list and playlist content shown on YouTube pages, such as video IDs, titles, channel names, watched state, and playlist ordering. This information is used only to provide the selection, filtering, copying, moving, grouping, reordering, and removal features you request.

Preferences and interface state are stored locally in the browser. This can include the action-bar position, display settings, filter settings, onboarding progress, and usage counters.

In short: playlist information is used to perform the action you choose, and your preferences stay in your browser.

Information we do not collect

Youtube Multiselect does not operate a remote analytics service, sell personal information, or transfer user data to third parties. It does not collect passwords, payment information, messages, health information, or precise location data.

The extension also does not use playlist content to build a separate profile about you. It interacts with YouTube only to provide the controls you have enabled and the actions you have requested.

Permissions

Storage: saves local preferences and interface state so the extension can remember how you use it.

YouTube and YouTube Music access: displays the extension controls and performs actions requested by you using your existing signed-in session. The extension does not ask for your YouTube password or handle your authentication credentials.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy or the extension, visit the project issue tracker. You can also review the implementation in the source repository.
