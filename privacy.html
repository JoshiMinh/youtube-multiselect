Privacy Policy

Youtube Multiselect · Last updated: October 31, 2026

What the extension does

Youtube Multiselect adds multi-selection and batch-management tools to YouTube and YouTube Music video lists and playlists.

Information processed

The extension temporarily processes video-list and playlist content shown on YouTube pages, such as video IDs, titles, channel names, watched state, and playlist ordering. This information is used only to provide the requested selection, filtering, copying, moving, grouping, reordering, and removal features.

Preferences and interface state are stored locally in the browser, including action-bar position, display settings, filter settings, onboarding progress, and usage counters.

Information we do not collect

Youtube Multiselect does not operate a remote analytics service, sell personal information, or transfer user data to third parties. It does not collect passwords, payment information, messages, health information, or precise location data.

Permissions

Storage permission saves local preferences. Access to YouTube and YouTube Music is required to display the extension controls and perform actions that the user requests using their existing signed-in session.
