A better way to manage lists

More videos. Less repetition.

Youtube Multiselect brings practical batch tools to YouTube and YouTube Music, so managing a playlist feels as quick as managing one video.

Built for real playlists

The controls you need, without leaving YouTube.

Select videos individually, by filter, or across an entire playlist. Then apply an action to the selection in one place.

01

Find a precise selection

Filter by title, channel, watched progress, duration, or position. Search forward or backward, including duplicates.

02

Act on many videos

Copy, cut, paste, queue, save, remove, or download a group of videos instead of repeating the same action.

03

Keep your workflow close

Use keyboard shortcuts, a floating action bar, and optional playlist tools that follow YouTube's light and dark themes.

A simple rhythm

Select, shape, finish.

01 · Select

Choose one video, a filtered group, or the whole playlist.

02 · Shape

Copy, reorder, group by channel, or move the selection where it belongs.

03 · Finish

Save your changes, clear the selection, and get back to watching.
